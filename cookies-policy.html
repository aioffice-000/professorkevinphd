This Cookies Policy explains how Professor Kevin, Ph.D. uses cookies and similar technologies on this website and how you may manage your cookie preferences.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help improve website functionality, support user preferences, and allow us to understand how visitors interact with our content.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the proper operation of the website, including security and basic functionality.
  • Preference cookies: Used to remember user selections and settings.
  • Analytics cookies: Used to understand how the website is used and to improve performance and content.

Your Choices

You may accept or decline non-essential cookies through the cookie banner displayed on our website. You may also manage cookie settings in your browser or device settings at any time, subject to the limitations of such settings.

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party tools and analytics providers may place cookies on your device to help us measure website performance and improve user experience. We do not sell personal information collected through cookies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.
