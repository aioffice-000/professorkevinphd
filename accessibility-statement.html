Professor Kevin, Ph.D. is committed to providing a website that is accessible to individuals with disabilities and to improving the overall digital experience for all users.

Accessibility Commitment

We aim to design and maintain our website using accessible practices, including clear navigation, readable text, sufficient color contrast, and compatibility with commonly used assistive technologies such as screen readers and keyboard navigation.

Accessibility Features

  • Logical page structure and descriptive headings
  • Readable text and scalable content
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation where feasible
  • Clear links and content organization

Continuous Improvement

We review and update the website on an ongoing basis to improve accessibility and usability. We welcome feedback regarding any barriers or difficulties encountered while using the site.

Contact

If you require assistance accessing any part of our website or need to report an accessibility issue, please contact us at hello@professorkevinphd.com.
